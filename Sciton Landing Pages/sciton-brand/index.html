The platform

Everything you need in one powerhouse platform.

JOULE is Sciton's expandable platform: over ten treatment modules and two distinct delivery modes on a single chassis.

Most clinics buy a device for one indication, then buy again eighteen months later when demand shifts. JOULE is built the other way round. Start with the modules that match today's patient list, then add applications as the clinic grows — without replacing the platform, retraining on a new interface, or finding floor space for a second system.

Every module runs from the same software, the same footprint and the same service contract. One capital purchase, one supplier, one relationship.

The commercial case

Did you know JOULE owners average more than 3x their monthly lease payment?

A single-application device earns from one indication. A modular platform earns from several, and each module you add uses training, floor space and a service contract you have already paid for. That is the difference between a purchase that depreciates and one that compounds.
